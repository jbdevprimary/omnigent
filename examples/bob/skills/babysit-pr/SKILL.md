Drive a PR through the target repository's actual merge requirements.

Inspect the PR's repository, base, head, effective rules, and current status
using the main prompt's Merge ownership procedure. Act on required checks,
reviews, conversations, and queue requirements as applicable. Fix actionable
failures and recheck after changes. Use the repository's allowed merge method
or queue and verify the resulting state. Report actual blockers; do not add
a review, thread-resolution, or approval gate of your own.
