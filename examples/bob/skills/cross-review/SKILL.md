Get a second opinion on a change when useful or required by external policy.

Choose a reviewer and scope appropriate to the change. Use a separate vendor
only if requested, required, or useful. Share the diff or checkout, acceptance
criteria, and current head. Ask for evidence and actionable findings. Use the
findings to decide what to fix or verify. This skill adds no merge requirement.
